# Consumer price index (IPC), Spain — annual average change (%)

annual average. Values 2023–2025 checked against Funcas, "IPC diciembre 2025",
cuadro 2 (https://www.funcas.es/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/IPC2512.pdf);
series 2010–2025 as published by the Centro Regional de Estadística de Murcia,
IPC anual 2025, sección 5
(https://econet.carm.es/inicio/-/crem/sicrem/PU_IPC/anual_25/pdf/sec5.pdf).

Update each January when the INE publishes the December CPI.
